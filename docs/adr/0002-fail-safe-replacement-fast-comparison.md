# Fail-safe replacement and two-second Fast comparison

Direct overwrite risks destroying a good Destination after a failed or cancelled transfer, so SnapSync streams into a unique temporary file beside the Destination, flushes and finalizes it, preserves Source LastWriteTimeUtc, and replaces the target only after successful staging and a Source length/time stability check. Fast comparison uses equal length and an inclusive two-second UTC modification-time tolerance to accommodate FAT/exFAT granularity; this trades detection of equal-size changes inside that window for inexpensive comparison, with SHA-256 available when content equality matters. Failed or cancelled transfers preserve the old Destination, attempt cleanup of only their own temporary file, and do not retry; creation times, ACLs, and attributes are not preserved.
